How the rescue process works

1
Request

A person submits a rescue request with location, people and urgency.

2
Prioritise

The system scores the request so urgent cases appear first for human review.

3
Assign

An administrator assigns an available rescue team.

4
Rescue & shelter

Progress is recorded and people are guided to a shelter with space.

Emergency preparedness tips

  • Know your nearest shelter and two ways to reach it.
  • Keep an emergency kit: water, food, torch, power bank, medicines.
  • Store documents in a waterproof bag.
  • Move to higher ground early when authorities advise it.
  • Never walk or drive through flood water.
  • Switch off electricity at the main switch if water enters your home.
